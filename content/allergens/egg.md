## The basics

Egg allergy affects about 2% of young children and is one of the most
common childhood food allergies. The reaction is to egg proteins — most
notably **ovalbumin** in the white and **livetin** in the yolk — not to
the cholesterol or fat.

Most egg-allergic children outgrow the allergy, often by ages 5-8. Tolerance
usually develops in a predictable order: baked egg first (cookies, muffins
that hit high heat for long enough), then less-cooked forms (pancakes,
French toast), then scrambled and finally raw.

## Reading labels

FALCPA requires "egg" to be declared in plain language. Look for:

- **Egg, egg white, egg yolk, dried egg, powdered egg**
- **Albumin, albumen, ovalbumin, ovomucin, ovomucoid, livetin**
- **Lysozyme** (an egg-derived enzyme used in some cheeses and wines)
- **Mayo, mayonnaise, aioli, hollandaise, meringue**
- **Egg substitutes** — despite the name, most contain egg whites; check
  the label

## Hidden sources

Egg can hide in:

- **Pasta** (some Italian pastas use egg; many American boxed pastas don't)
- **Bagels, pretzels, and shiny-topped breads** (egg wash is common)
- **Foam on cappuccinos and lattes** (some shops use egg whites for foam art)
- **Marshmallows, meringue, soufflés**
- **Battered, breaded, or coated foods** (egg is often the binder)
- **Wines and beers** (egg whites are used as a fining/clarifying agent)
- **Some fresh and frozen pastas, tortellini, ravioli**

## Daily life

**At home:** Most egg-allergic households keep eggs out for the first few
years, then reintroduce as part of the baked-egg ladder under their
allergist's guidance.

**At school:** Watch for egg-based crafts (papier-mâché, painted eggs at
holidays), egg cartons used for art projects, and breakfast offerings.
Many cafeterias use egg in unexpected places.

**Vaccines:** Most flu vaccines today are either made entirely without eggs
(recombinant or cell-based) or contain such trace amounts that they're
considered safe for egg-allergic patients. The CDC and most allergy
organizations no longer recommend skipping flu shots because of egg
allergy — but confirm with your allergist before each year's shot.
The MMR vaccine, despite being grown in chicken cells, has not been shown
to cause reactions in egg-allergic children.

**Eating out:** Brunch menus and bakeries are highest-risk. Most Asian
cuisine uses some egg in noodles and dumplings. Always ask.

## Treatments

**Egg oral immunotherapy** (OIT) is offered at specialty allergy centers
and often follows the baked-egg ladder. **Omalizumab** (Xolair) was
approved in 2024 for multiple food allergies including egg, raising the
threshold for accidental-exposure reactions.

Both are personal decisions made with an allergist who specializes in food
allergy.

## Questions to ask your allergist

- Are we ready for the baked-egg ladder? What's the safest way to start?
- Is my child likely to outgrow this? What do their numbers suggest?
- Is the flu vaccine safe for us this season?
- Are we candidates for OIT or omalizumab?
- What's the threshold for using epinephrine after accidental exposure?
